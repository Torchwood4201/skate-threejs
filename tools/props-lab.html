THE LIBRARY — every factory in src/world/procedural/, built here, stood on the spot's own concrete, lit by the game's OWN rig (createField's sun, hemisphere, fog and sky — not a copy of its numbers), tone-mapped the way main.ts does it. Cameras are at skater eye height and 4–15 m out, which is where a chase camera sees a prop from. Click a tile to blow it up.
THE PRINT ATLAS — every legend in the library on one 512² page, one material. If a sign reads wrong in a tile, look here first: it is either the cell or the UVs, and this tells you which.
THE LINE-UP — all of them together from the SHADE side, so scale, silhouette and the shadow-side read can be judged against each other. The striped pole is 1.75 m, a rider.
THE CONTACT SHEET — each factory at several seeds, from the SUN side. Two seeds that look the same are a bug.
THE NUMBERS — cost per factory, the stated envelope against the real geometry, and the COLLIDER GATE. The collider is the only number in the library the physics reads, and no picture can check it: a landing surface twenty centimetres above a cabinet roof looks exactly like one on it. So every rideable prop gets a grid of rays dropped through its own collider footprint, and the row is red unless the height the board would rest at is the height of the geometry under it. It asks for that collider through both spellings the two levels use and fails the row if they are not the same object — this page was 100% green for weeks while the street it was green about was 34/41 wrong, because it asked one and the game asked the other.
